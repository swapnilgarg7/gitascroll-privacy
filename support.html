Common questions

I can't hear anything. Check the speaker icon at the top right is not muted and your volume is up. GitaScroll plays even when your iPhone's silent switch is on.

How do I see what a Sanskrit word means? Tap any underlined Sanskrit word on a verse, or tap "Words" on the right for the full word-for-word meaning.

Where are my saved verses? Open the library (the list icon at the top) and choose "Saved". Saved verses are stored only on your device.

When are new verses added? Regularly. New verses arrive automatically the next time you open the app.
