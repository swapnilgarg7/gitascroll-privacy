GitaScroll Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026. Applies to the GitaScroll app for Android and iOS (package com.gitascroll.app), published by SWAPNIL TECH.

GitaScroll lets you scroll through verses of the Bhagavad-gita with narration, music and animation. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

What we collect

To understand which verses people enjoy and where the app can improve, the app sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog. These include:

  • App activity: which verse played, how far you watched it, whether you replayed or skipped it, Sanskrit words you tapped, quiz answers, and when you liked, saved or shared a verse or opened the library.
  • A random identifier created on your device when the app is first opened. It is not linked to your name, Google account or advertising ID.
  • Basic technical details: device model, operating system version, app version, screen size and language.
  • Your IP address, which PostHog receives with each request and may use to estimate your country and city. We do not collect precise location.

What stays on your device

Your likes, saved verses and sound setting are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

How we use it

Only to measure and improve GitaScroll: for example, which verses are watched to the end and which features are used. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the service providers below.

Service providers

  • PostHog (analytics), with data stored in the United States. PostHog privacy policy.
  • Expo (app updates). When the app checks for new content, Expo's servers receive a standard network request including your IP address. Expo privacy policy.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Retention and deletion

Analytics events are kept for up to 2 years and then deleted. Because events are anonymous, you can ask us to delete them by emailing us; uninstalling the app also stops all collection and resets the random identifier.

Children

GitaScroll is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
